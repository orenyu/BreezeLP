
## Product Information Form Specification

1.	Your Product Field
•	Dropdown menu with the following options:MedTech, AgTech, High-Tech, Industrial, Military
•	Includes "Other" option – when selected, a free text field opens

2.	Describe Your Product Shortly
•	Short text box for a one-sentence product description

3.	Up to 3 Main Product Benefits
•	Multiple-selection dropdown (up to 3 selections)
•	Includes "Other" option

4.	Up to 3 Secondary Product Benefits
•	Multiple-selection dropdown (up to 3 selections)
•	Includes "Other" option

5.	Challenges
•	Text box – for example: regulatory constraints, budget, timeline, operations

6.	What is the Primary Goal of the Content?
•	Single-selection dropdown with the following options: Product launch, Brand awareness, Explain complex technology, Investor pitch, Increase sales

7.	Who is the Target Audience?
•	Multiple-selection dropdown with "Other" option
•	Includes Military as a fixed option

8.	3 Main Messages
•	Three short input fields

9.	3 Secondary Messages
•	Three short input fields

10.	Website link
•	Website URL – Link field
